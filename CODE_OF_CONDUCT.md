# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and patient. People here come from different backgrounds and
  have different levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or
  anywhere else connected to the project.

If someone's behaviour is a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on
the comment, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may block people who keep breaking
these rules.
