# Changelog

## 1.7
- Menu: "Remind me every" (15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes, or any number from 1 to 240).
  Changing it restarts the count.
- Menu: "Reminder stays on screen" (15 or 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes).

## 1.6
- During a call the menu shows one plain sentence and the app's name instead of its
  bundle identifier.

## 1.5
- App icon: a figure stretching on a green tile.
- About window with the owner's name.
- Menu: "Change reminder message…".

## 1.4
- Live countdown to the next reminder, next to the menu bar icon (optional) and in the menu.

## 1.3
- "Show reminder now" follows the same rule as a real reminder: during a call or a Focus
  it explains why it is waiting, with a "Show it anyway" button.
- Each "Show reminder now" writes what the detectors saw to the log.
- The menu says when a call or Focus is being detected.

## 1.2
- Icon is green when on and gray when off.
- Locking the screen or sleeping switches reminders off until turned on again
  (like Caffeine). Can be unticked in the menu.

## 1.1
- Menu bar icon and menu: turn off, turn off for 1 hour, show reminder now, open log,
  uninstall, quit.
- "Start at login" switch.
- Installs into /Applications. Only one copy can run.

## 1.0
- First version: full-screen reminder after 30 minutes of sitting, pauses media,
  stays quiet during a Focus, a call, and while locked or asleep.
- Installer commands: install, test, preview, status, restart, uninstall.
